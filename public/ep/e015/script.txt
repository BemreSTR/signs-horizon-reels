For ten years, one sentence could have destroyed Islam.

In Mecca, a revelation declared that the Prophet's fiercest enemy, Abu Lahab, would burn in blazing fire.

He was condemned while still alive.

Abu Lahab lived for ten more years. Over three thousand six hundred days.

He didn't need an army, and he didn't need a sword. All he had to do was publicly utter four words, even as a lie: "I accept your faith."

The door was wide open. Yet the man who dedicated his life to defeating the message... never walked through it.

An impostor never hands his enemy the key to his own destruction. Because for ten years, one sentence could have destroyed Islam.
