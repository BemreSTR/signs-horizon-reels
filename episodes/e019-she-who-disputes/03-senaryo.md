# E019 · Senaryo (sesli) · She Who Disputes

> Ses: "Emin - Epic Historical Narrative" (PVC), Eleven v4, hız 1.20, Stability 45, Similarity 100 (kanalın sabit sesi, doc 03 §7). 208 kelime ≈ 77 sn konuşma + sessizlikler + 3 sn outro ≈ 85 sn. `{{silence:x}}` ElevenLabs metnine girmez, miksajda eklenir.
> Ayet Arapçası seslendirilmez; ekranda gösterilir (58:1, Quran.com `text_uthmani`). Seslendirilen çeviri bizim çevirimiz.

¶1 {{hook}} A woman argued with a Prophet. A chapter was named after her. {{silence:0.6}}

¶2 {{medina}} Medina. Her name was {{khawla}} Khawla. In anger, her husband said one sentence: you are to me like my mother's back. {{silence:0.4}}

¶3 {{trap}} By their custom, those words were a trap. She was not divorced. She was not a wife. She was NOWHERE. {{silence:0.6}}

¶4 {{limbo}} The reports say he had spent her youth, then left her in limbo.

¶5 {{door}} She went to Muhammad, peace be upon him. {{fear_allah}} At first he said: fear Allah. He is your cousin. {{no_revelation}} No revelation had come yet. {{silence:0.9}}

¶6 {{stays}} She did not leave. She turned her complaint to Allah. {{aisha}} Aisha, in the same room, could not catch every word. {{silence:0.8}} {{heard}} But Allah heard all of it. {{silence:0.8}}

¶7 {{verse}} Then came the verse: Allah has heard the woman who argues with you about her husband and complains to Allah. And Allah hears the two of you talking. {{silence:1.2}}

¶8 {{wrong}} The Quran calls his words a wrong and a falsehood. {{atone}} His path back was atonement: free a slave, fast two months, or feed sixty poor people. {{silence:0.6}}

¶9 {{chapter_name}} The chapter is called Al-Mujadila: She Who Disputes. {{reported}} Her story is reported. The verses are the Quran. {{silence:0.8}}

¶10 {{queen}} She was not a queen. She was not a scholar. {{asking}} She was a woman who would not stop asking. Allah kept her question.

> Dürüst mercek ¶4, ¶5 ("No revelation had come yet"), ¶9 ("reported"). "Evlilik bitti" denmez; ¶8 yalnızca kefaretle dönüşü söyler.
